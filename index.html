Paul's Ice Cream House 🍦

Welcome to a house where everything is made of ice cream, candy & sprinkles!

It's a warm day, so the treats are starting to melt. 🫠 Point your Freeze Gun at anything and hold to freeze it — frozen treats get a shiny ❄️ snowflake and stay cool for a long time, so you can go freeze more! Grab floating power-ups to make your gun stronger. Watch out for the 💧 drip — that treat needs a re-freeze soon.

Phone: left stick to walk · slide the right side to look · hold FREEZE.
Computer: WASD/arrows to walk · drag the mouse to look · hold Space to freeze.
Have fun exploring every room!
