Paul's Ice Cream House 🍦

Welcome to a house where the walls, floors and furniture are all made of ice cream!

It's a warm day, so the house is starting to melt. 🫠 Point your Freeze Gun and hold to freeze things solid — frozen treats get a shiny ❄️ snowflake and stay cool a long time. Rooms only start melting once you walk into them, so explore! Grab floating power-ups to boost your gun, and watch for the 💧 drip — that needs a re-freeze.

Phone: left stick to walk · slide the right side to look · hold FREEZE.
Computer: WASD/arrows to walk · drag the mouse to look · hold Space.
Have fun exploring every room!
