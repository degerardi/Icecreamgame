Paul's Ice Cream House 🍦

Welcome to a house where the walls, floors and furniture are all made of ice cream!

You're a 🌈 rainbow dragon in a house that's cool and safe — until someone tips the 🏆 trophy! Knock over the trophy in the dining area to kick off the heat, then walk up to treats and hold FREEZE to breathe frost on them. Frozen treats get a shiny ❄️ snowflake and stay cool a long time. Rooms only start melting once you walk into them, so explore! Grab floating power-ups to boost your frost, and watch for the 💧 drip — that needs a re-freeze. Stand the trophy back up any time to re-freeze everything and stop the melting. Out on the 🏖️ beach you can also walk up to the big 🚗 RC controller and drive a green dragon-badged monster truck — with blinking rainbow lights — anywhere on the sand, and flip it!

Phone: one joystick to walk anywhere · hold FREEZE to breathe frost.
Computer: WASD/arrows to walk · hold Space to breathe frost.
Have fun exploring every room!
